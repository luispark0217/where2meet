# Design QA: Figma vs. web (A–G)

Method: `npm run build`, then `python3 qa/compare.py` (390x844, `?frame=1`). I cropped and zoomed at 2–4x with PIL, measured edges from row/column pixel profiles, and sampled colours. Exact positions come from Figma `get_design_context` for B, D, E, F and G (5 calls). Frame y = Figma y + 8. The children of the map layers in D, E and F already use frame coordinates.
Excluded because they are intentional: status bar and bezel, grey photo placeholders (tone and gradient), the map background art in D/E/F, the tab icon squares, and the crown's redrawn shape.
Coordinates below are in the 390x844 screenshot (F = Figma, W = web).

## Verdict per screen
- **A (내 모임):** Close. One clear bug: the card photo frame is missing on the right (#1). Headline is 1px low.
- **B (모임 상세):** Close. The done card's crown and text sit 6px right (#9), mini map markers are small (#10), and there is a hero fade seam (#12).
- **C (약속 상세):** Very close. The time grid is pixel-exact. The midpoint marker colours are inverted (#3).
- **D (서울 왕좌 지도):** Very close. All markers, chips and controls are within 1px. Bottom card text is 2px low (#8).
- **E (홍대·연남):** Needs fixes. Carousel is offset 24px (#2), the "my" marker ring and glow are wrong (#4, #5), and card contents sit low (#7).
- **F (장소 · 모임 랭킹):** Very close. Layout matches Figma values exactly. Glow is too small (#5) and the 2위 crown has no outline (#11).
- **G (랭킹 리스트):** Close. White photo rings are missing on the list and podium (#6). Headline is 1px low.
